# Gym Training Library

Use the GYM Obsidian vault as the canonical external-source library. Keep source capture, evidence assessment, and personal application distinct.

## Load the Library

- Use `C:\Users\Tarlu\iCloudDrive\GYM` as the canonical vault root.
- Read `Training Source Library.md` before recalling or comparing stored sources.
- Read only the relevant `Source - *.md` notes unless a cross-source synthesis requires more.
- When asked whether advice fits Tarlu, also use `personal-gym-goal-coach` and read `Personal Profile.md`, `Current Status.md`, `Current Training Plan.md`, and relevant recent decisions.

## Ingest a Source

1. Canonicalize the URL and deduplicate by stable platform or publication ID.
2. Record title, creator, creator/channel ID when available, publication date, duration, access date, language, caption type, and commercial disclosures.
3. Use captions transiently. Mark automatic captions and spot-check exercise names, quantities, negations, and technical terms against the audiovisual source.
4. Inspect visual-only programming screens separately because captions may omit them.
5. Save a detailed timestamped paraphrase, not an unlabeled or reconstructed verbatim transcript.
6. Convert actionable advice into atomic claims with stable IDs.
7. Record population, exercise dose, intended outcome, equipment, limitations, safety concerns, and evidence the creator cites.
8. Check important claims against current authoritative guidance, systematic reviews, and relevant primary research.
9. Label evidence alignment as `supported`, `partly supported`, `context-dependent`, `unsupported`, `unverified`, or `opinion`.
10. Update `Training Source Library.md` and link the source note to `Training Decisions.md`.

## Separate Three Questions

Always distinguish:

1. **What did the creator claim?** Preserve a faithful paraphrase and timestamp.
2. **How well is the claim supported?** Evaluate independently; popularity and view count are not evidence.
3. **Is it appropriate for Tarlu now?** Consider beginner status, current weekly volume, recovery, health, available equipment, and the active program.

Do not let a source silently change `Current Training Plan.md`. A useful exercise will often replace an existing movement instead of merely adding fatigue.

## Record Personal Application

- Record `adopt`, `modify`, `reject`, or `test` decisions in `Training Decisions.md` only when Tarlu explicitly asks to save the decision or change the plan.
- Reference source claim IDs rather than copying the entire source digest.
- For a trial, record the exercise and substitution, sets, reps, RIR, trial window, success criteria, stop criteria, and result.
- Keep Tarlu's workout experience and results in the personal vault notes, not in the neutral source summary.

## Rights and Safety

- Do not retain or provide a complete third-party transcript merely from a link. Use timestamped paraphrases unless Tarlu owns the content, has permission, or provides an authorized transcript.
- Use only short quotations when genuinely necessary.
- Flag claims involving injury treatment, medication, supplements, dangerous rapid weight change, or alarming symptoms for appropriate professional review.
- Treat creator credentials, sponsorships, and conflicts as facts only when verified.
